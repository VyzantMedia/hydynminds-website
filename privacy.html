Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

2.1 What we collect

When you submit the contact form: your name, email, practice or business name, and whatever you write about your upcoming event. We don't collect anything else automatically unless analytics is added later (this policy will be updated first if so).

2.2 How it's used

Solely to respond to your inquiry and scope a potential project. Submissions are stored via our internal tools (Google Sheets, connected through an automation platform called n8n) — not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

2.3 Cross-border processing

Hydyn Minds operates from India. Submitting the contact form means your information is processed and stored outside your own country. By submitting the form, you consent to this transfer.

2.4 Retention

We keep inquiry data as long as reasonably useful for following up, or until you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first.

2.5 Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@hydynminds.in. We aim to respond within a reasonable time. (If you later have California or Canadian clients/attendees at meaningful volume, CCPA and PIPEDA both give data subjects similar rights — worth a lawyer's check on whether formal compliance obligations apply once you're at scale; this policy already reflects the spirit of both.)

2.6 Event footage and audio

Different from form data. Raw footage, audio, and photos you send us for production are not covered by this Privacy Policy — they're governed by the Client Service Agreement, because they're project materials under contract, not personal data collected through browsing. That agreement covers consent, ownership, and how that content can be used.

2.7 Cookies/analytics

None currently in use. This section will be updated if that changes.
